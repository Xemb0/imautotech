Frequently asked

What does "we handle every aspect" mean?
Hosting, monitoring, updates, bug fixes, store compliance, and security — the day-to-day running of your app or website so you don't have to think about it. Higher tiers add new features, dedicated developer time, and growth support.
Is the app development price really just ₹10,000?
That's the starting point for a focused build. Final pricing depends on the features, number of platforms, and integrations you need — we give you a fixed quote before any work begins.

Absolutely. Consultancy is billed per hour at ₹1,999 with no commitment. Book a single session or several — whatever you need.
