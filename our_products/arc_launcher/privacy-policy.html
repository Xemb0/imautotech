This Privacy Policy describes how Imautotech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Arc Launcher application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Device & Usage Information

We may collect information about your device and how you interact with the App, including device model, operating system version, app version, session duration, and feature usage patterns. This data helps us improve performance and user experience.

Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, and interaction data to deliver personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics Data

We use analytics services (including Google Analytics and Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with the App. This may include information such as crash reports, performance metrics, screen views, and user engagement data.

Permissions We Require

Arc Launcher is a home screen replacement application that enhances your Android experience. To provide this functionality, the App requires the following permissions:

Home Screen Access

Required to function as your default home screen launcher, managing app icons, widgets, and wallpapers on your home screen.

Storage Access

Used to load and display custom wallpapers, icon packs, and theme assets stored on your device.

App Usage Access

Used to provide smart app suggestions and organize frequently used applications for quick access on your home screen.

Internet Access

Used to serve advertisements via AdMob, send analytics data, check for app updates, and download theme resources.

Notification Access

Used to display notification badges on app icons, showing unread notification counts for each application.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the launcher functionality and home screen experience
  • To display smart app suggestions and personalized layouts
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze usage trends, diagnose technical issues, and improve the App
  • To send crash reports and performance data for troubleshooting

Third-Party Services

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
