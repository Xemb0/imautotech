This Privacy Policy describes how Imautotech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the WX Gamepad application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Device & Usage Information

We may collect information about your device and how you interact with the App, including device model, operating system version, app version, session duration, and feature usage patterns. This data helps us improve performance and user experience.

Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, and interaction data to deliver personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics Data

We use analytics services (including Google Analytics and Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with the App. This may include information such as crash reports, performance metrics, screen views, and user engagement data.

Permissions We Require

WX Gamepad is a virtual gamepad application that turns your mobile device into a wireless game controller. To provide this core functionality, the App requires the following permissions:

Wi-Fi Access

Required to discover and connect to the WX Gamepad server running on your PC or Mac over your local Wi-Fi network. The App uses Wi-Fi to establish a low-latency connection for transmitting controller inputs in real time.

Bluetooth Access

Used as an alternative connection method to pair your device with a host computer via Bluetooth. This enables wireless gamepad functionality even without a shared Wi-Fi network.

Network State / Wireless Connection

Required to detect available networks, check connectivity status, and ensure a stable connection between your device and the game server. This is essential for maintaining responsive gamepad controls during gameplay.

Internet Access

Used to serve advertisements via AdMob, send analytics data, check for app updates, and facilitate QR code-based connection setup.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the virtual gamepad functionality
  • To establish and manage wireless connections between your device and game servers
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze usage trends, diagnose technical issues, and improve the App
  • To send crash reports and performance data for troubleshooting
