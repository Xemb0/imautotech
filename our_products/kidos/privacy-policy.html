KidOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

KidOS is a self-contained, walled-garden "phone" experience for young children, made by Imautotech ("we", "us", "our"). It was engineered to collect as close to nothing as possible: there are no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no social features that leave the device.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no personal profiles. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone.
  • No ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The in-app "Call", "Message", "Friends", and "Internet" sections are pretend, fully local experiences — nothing is transmitted to other people and there is no real telephony, messaging, or open web access.
  • Settings sit behind a parental gate a young child cannot pass.

What the App Handles

Camera Photos

The sticker camera uses the front camera (with your permission) so the child can take fun photos with animal friends. Photos never leave the device — they are stored inside the app only, are not uploaded anywhere, and are not added to the system photo library.

Recordings & Creations

Instrument recordings and game progress are kept locally on the device.

Downloadable Content

Games and stories are downloaded from our servers as ordinary anonymous web requests. No account or identifier is attached to them.

Push Notifications

If notifications are allowed, an anonymous device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) is registered so we can send occasional content updates. The token is not linked to any person. Notifications can be disabled in device settings at any time.

We do not collect: names, emails, location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Service Providers

  • Supabase — hosts the downloadable games and stories catalogue (privacy policy)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — push delivery only; we do not use Firebase Analytics (privacy policy)

Permissions

Camera

Optional. Used only for the sticker camera's live preview and photos, which stay on the device. Deny it and the rest of the app works normally.

Notifications

Optional. Used for occasional content update announcements.

Your Choices & Deletion

Because KidOS keeps its data on the device, deleting the app deletes the data — photos, recordings, downloads, and settings. Push tokens become inactive automatically. For any request, contact support@imautotech.in.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
