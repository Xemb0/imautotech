KidOS Has No Accounts

KidOS does not use accounts or sign-in, and does not store personal data on our servers. Everything the app creates — photos, drawings, recordings, downloaded games and stories, and settings — lives on your device only.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Delete the KidOS app from your device (long-press the icon → Remove/Uninstall)
  2. That's it — all photos, drawings, recordings, downloads, and settings are permanently removed with the app

The only server-side record is an anonymous push notification token (not linked to any person), which becomes inactive automatically once the app is deleted. If you would like it proactively removed, email us and mention roughly when you deleted the app.
