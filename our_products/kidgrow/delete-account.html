Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your KidGrow parent account and all data linked to it at any time. Deletion is permanent.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your parent account (email / Google / Apple sign-in identifier)
  • All child profiles (name, age, gender, country, language, avatar)
  • Push notification tokens linked to your account

Learning progress stored on your device is removed when you delete the app itself.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@imautotech.in with the subject "Delete my KidGrow account"
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in (or tell us that address in the message) so we can verify it's you
  3. We will confirm and delete the account and all associated data within 30 days — usually much sooner
Email a Deletion Request

Just Want to Stop Using KidGrow?

You can simply sign out (Grown-up zone → Sign out) and delete the app. If you only want to reset your child's learning progress, use Grown-up zone → Reset progress — no need to delete the account.
