Frequently asked questions

My child got out of the app / into the wrong section — how does Kid Mode work?

When Kid Mode is locked (the green lock in the top corner), Settings and other grown-up areas are protected by a math question only grown-ups can answer. To lock or unlock, tap the lock pill in the status bar.

Tip: you can also enable iOS Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) for an extra layer that keeps the home button/gestures locked too.

How do I turn sections (Camera, Videos, Games…) on or off?

Open Settings from the dock (it's behind the parental gate), then toggle any section. The change appears on your child's home screen immediately.

How do pretend Calls and Messages work? Is my child contacting anyone real?

No. Calls and Messages are fully pretend — there is no real network, dialing, or chat. Your child "calls" only the trusted grown-ups you add in Settings, and the conversation is simulated on the device.

How does the learning adapt to my child?

KidGrow uses spaced repetition: cards your child knows well appear less often, and the ones they mix up come back sooner. The Grown-ups Zone shows exactly what's known, what's being learned, and which pairs get confused.

How do I manage or cancel my KidGrow Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → KidGrow, where you can change or cancel anytime. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the paid period.

To restore a purchase on a new device: Grown-ups Zone → Restore purchases.

Does KidGrow show ads or talk to strangers?

Never. KidGrow has no third-party ads, no chats with real people, and nothing your child can stumble into. The pretend Internet and Friends sections are entirely on-device.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at support@imautotech.in from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete your account and associated data within 30 days — or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Learning progress stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Something's not working — sound, speech, or a frozen screen.

First try: check the ring/silent switch and volume, then force-quit and reopen the app. If speech is silent, make sure the speaker button in the app is on (yellow).

Still stuck? Email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll dig in.
